Lahore

Lahore is one of Pakistan's most famous food cities. It is especially known for its rich, spicy and hearty Punjabi cuisine and its lively food streets. Food is an important part of Lahore's culture. Areas such as the Walled City are famous for traditional restaurants and street food. Lahore is also well known for its traditional breakfasts and barbecue dishes..

Karachi

Karachi, the largest city in Pakistan, is famous for its incredibly diverse food scene. Because people from many different regions and communities live in Karachi, the city has developed a huge variety of cuisines. Karachi is particularly famous for biryani, spicy street food, barbecue and traditional Sindhi dishes.

Peshawar

Peshawar is one of the oldest cities in the region and is particularly famous for its Pashtun food culture. Compared with some Punjabi and Sindhi dishes, traditional Peshawari cuisine often focuses more on meat, simple seasonings, natural flavors and grilling.
